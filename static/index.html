FREE · OPEN SOURCE · YOUR MUSIC STAYS LOCAL

A music downloader built around your library.

Sonkip is a free, open-source music downloader for Android, Windows, macOS and Linux. Search for public music or paste a supported song, playlist or album link, review the result, then save the tracks you choose. Sonkip keeps the audio and library database on your device instead of creating another online account or cloud collection.

It is designed for people who want an organized offline music library, not a folder full of mystery files. When reliable public information is available, Sonkip preserves the song title, primary artist, album, album artist, track number, release date, genre and cover artwork. That metadata helps Android players and desktop music apps group full albums correctly.

01

Review playlists before downloading

A pasted playlist or album opens as a track list first. Select all, remove uncertain matches or save only a few songs. Nothing enters the download queue until you confirm the selection.

02

Cleaner albums and metadata

Release identity, album artist, track order and shared artwork keep tracks from the same album together. Sonkip avoids inventing an album when trustworthy album information is not available.

03

Private and local by design

There is no Sonkip account, advertising profile, analytics tracker or hosted cloud library. The Android app stores music in app-private storage, while the desktop service listens only on your own computer.

04

Android and desktop playback

Play saved tracks offline, create local playlists, shuffle the library and keep downloads running in the background on Android. Desktop users can choose Original, MP3, M4A, Opus or FLAC output.

HOW SONKIP WORKS

From a public link to an organized local library.

  1. Find a song or paste a link. Search by song, artist or album, or paste a supported public YouTube, YouTube Music, Spotify or SoundCloud playlist link.
  2. Check every match. Sonkip prioritizes exact and official-looking matches, shows the complete preview and lets you change the selection before saving.
  3. Save and listen locally. New tracks enter your on-device library with available artwork and metadata. Previously saved music continues to play offline.

ONE PROJECT · FOUR PLATFORMS

Download Sonkip for Android or run it on desktop.

Install the APK on Android 7 or newer, use one-command setup on Windows, or run the macOS and Linux installer. The public website explains the project and selects the right download; the music downloader itself runs locally on your device.

QUESTIONS, ANSWERED

Sonkip music downloader FAQ

Is Sonkip free and open source?

Yes. Sonkip is free software released under the MIT License. The source code, issue tracker and Android APK releases are public on GitHub.

Which devices does Sonkip support?

Sonkip supports Android 7 or newer on ARM devices. The local desktop version runs on Windows, macOS and Linux.

Can Sonkip download a playlist or full album?

Yes. Supported public playlists and albums open in a review screen first. You choose the tracks, then press Save selected to begin downloading.

Does Sonkip download directly from Spotify or SoundCloud?

No. Sonkip reads available public track information, searches for possible YouTube matches and asks you to review those matches. Versions can differ, and unmatched tracks are reported instead of guessed.

Does it add album artwork and music metadata?

When reliable details are available, Sonkip can embed title, artist, album, album artist, track number, year, genre and cover artwork in new downloads.

Is Sonkip an online download service?

No. The website is the project landing page. Search, download, playback and library storage happen in the Android app or on your own desktop computer.

Use Sonkip only for public content you own or have permission to download, and follow the source platform's terms. Sonkip does not bypass DRM, paywalls or provider permissions. Source availability and public metadata can change over time.
